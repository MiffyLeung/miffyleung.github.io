CantoMore / Cantonese learning & cultural initiative

More than learning words.
Finding a way to participate.

CantoMore supports newly arrived Mainland Chinese students. Its concept evolved from Cantonese learning toward cultural connection and participation in Hong Kong.

My role
Co-founder & Tech Lead
When
Nov 2025 – Present
Stage
Initiative & early-stage product planning
ContextContributionEvidenceReflection

Context

A broader frame for the need

CantoMore began with Cantonese learning and grew toward cultural connection and participation. That repositioning made me look beyond an isolated lesson and consider the contexts in which people would use what they learn.

Contribution

My contribution within the team

I lead curriculum design and contribute to early-stage product planning. I translate learner needs into learning journeys, face-to-face activities, game-based concepts and supporting digital tools. The wider concept and trade-offs are developed collaboratively with my co-founders.

Evidence

Team decisions and external recognition

We refine the concept together through feedback and trade-offs around learning value, feasibility and potential school adoption. The initiative received a Grand Award in the Tertiary Division of the Hang Seng x HKFYG Seek Our Ways Ideation Programme (2nd Cohort), and Third Prize in the 12th Hong Kong University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition. The recognition encouraged further development; understanding the experience in practice remains an ongoing task.

Reflection

What repositioning changed for me

Understanding the wider system helped change how I described the initiative and what experiences might matter. It also raised a scope question: which part of that system can our team responsibly influence and test first?

See the bigger context. Still choose what the first experience needs to do.

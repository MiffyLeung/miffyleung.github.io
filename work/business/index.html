Inno / ProConnect / Business & service model exploration

An interface is not
the whole business.

Two projects expanded my attention from the immediate experience to delivery, business models and whether an idea can scale.

My role
Concept design & team coordination
When
Jan – Jul 2025 / Feb – May 2025
Stage
Concepts & demonstrable prototypes
ContextContributionEvidenceReflection

Context

Inno: career exploration

Inno explored an AI talent–role matching platform as part of the YDC Dare To Change Business Pitch Competition 2024–25. I designed an AI-assisted career-exploration prototype and refined the candidate-industry matching concept and scope in response to judges’ feedback.

Contribution

ProConnect: work exposure

ProConnect explored work exposure support for under-networked students within Impact Innovation Lab Cohort 7. I coordinated a cross-disciplinary team to map user journeys and develop business and service models, built a demonstrable interface with AI-assisted development tools, and incorporated reviewer feedback into revised documentation.

Evidence

Where the work reached

Both projects informed my understanding of business models and scalability. The supplied information establishes concepts, prototypes and feedback-driven revisions — not launched, profitable or commercially validated businesses.

Reflection

The question behind the screen

A useful interaction still relies on people, delivery and resources around it. These experiences made business and service models part of what I ask about when exploring a product.

Design the experience and the conditions that could make it viable.

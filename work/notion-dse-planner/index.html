01 / THE ARTIFACT

A planning resource.
Made to be reused.

I created a Notion DSE planner and shared it as a giveaway through ToLearner. This project is the template itself: a practical planning resource within a wider student-support community.

It is distinct from the Poe chatbot. The two share a community context, but they are not presented as a single integrated product.

Explore the Notion template ↗

02 / COMMUNITY CONTEXT

The community came first.

ToLearner was my first social enterprise project, started from my experience of having fewer resources. Its purpose was broader than a website or a planning tool: I wanted to support students with similar experiences.

The Notion template is one tangible contribution to that mission. The chatbot, web platform and community work are related, but they do not stand in for this project’s specific work.

Read the ToLearner community story →

03 / WHAT I SEE DIFFERENTLY NOW

The tool can be smaller
than the mission.

I used to equate a good product with covering every pain point of a target audience. Today, I would give a reusable planner one clear purpose before expanding its scope.

↳

More structure is not automatically more support. I would first ask which part of planning students need help with.

This describes how I would approach a next iteration, not a completed template-testing result.

04 / SCOPE & NEXT QUESTIONS

A shared template.
Its own evaluation questions.

The template is linked as an artifact to inspect. ToLearner’s 1,000+ students served describes the overall platform, not the number of template users, copies or completed plans.

NEXT QUESTION

What do students keep using?

I would look at which parts they return to, adapt or leave unused before deciding whether more structure belongs in the template.
